Privacy Policy — BudgetTracker

Last updated: 2026-06-30

BudgetTracker ("the app") is provided by Martin Antas. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, used for sign-in and syncing your data.
  • Financial data you enter: transactions, categories, budgets, recurring items, your hourly rate / monthly salary, and currency preference.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers for advertising. We do not sell your data.

How data is stored

Your data is stored in a secured Supabase (PostgreSQL) backend and protected by row-level security, so each account can only access its own data. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Shared account with TimeTracker

If you also use our TimeTracker app with the same account, both apps read from the same secured database so your worked hours and earnings can be shown in BudgetTracker. No data is shared with any third party.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Notifications

The app schedules local notifications on your device (payment reminders, budget alerts). These are generated on-device and are not sent through any server.

Data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account. This action is irreversible.
