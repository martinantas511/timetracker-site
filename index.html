Privacy Policy — TimeTracker

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TimeTracker mobile application ("the app", "we") handles your data. The app is operated by TimeTracker ("the developer").

Your account

You can start using the app without creating an account. In that case the app creates an anonymous guest account: your data is stored with us, but it is not linked to an email address. If you later sign in with Google or with an email address, your existing data is kept and linked to that account. If you log out of a guest account, its data can no longer be accessed.

Your TimeTracker account is shared with our sibling apps BudgetTracker and HabitTracker: the same sign-in works in all three.

What data we collect

  • Account data: your email address (if you sign in with email or Google), and, if you choose to add them, your name and a profile photo.
  • Google sign-in: if you use it, Google shares your name and email address with us. We do not receive your Google password or access your other Google data.
  • Your content: the clients, projects, time entries and invoices you create. Client records can contain the details you enter, such as name, email, address, IBAN and company / tax IDs.
  • Invoice details: the supplier details you enter for your invoices (name, address, company ID, tax and VAT IDs, IBAN, BIC, VAT settings), stored with your account so they survive a reinstall.
  • Usage events: to understand where new users get stuck, the app records a small set of anonymous steps in our own database (for example: app opened, account created, first project created, timer started, invoice created, paywall shown, purchase). Each event contains the step name, the app version and a random installation ID created by the app. It contains no content you entered, no location and no advertising ID.

We do not collect your location, contacts or device identifiers for advertising. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How your data is stored

  • Your account, content and usage events are stored in our backend provider Supabase (PostgreSQL database in the EU, with row-level security). Each user can only access their own data; usage events cannot be read by any app user.
  • Profile photos are stored in Supabase Storage.
  • Some preferences (language, Pomodoro, goal and reminder settings) are stored locally on your device only.

Payments

Pro purchases are processed by Google Play Billing and managed through RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat processes a purchase identifier linked to your account ID to verify your Pro status.

Notifications

The app may send local notifications: a running-timer notification, a reminder if a timer has been running for a long time, an optional daily reminder, Pomodoro phase alerts and a weekly summary of your tracked time. They are generated on your device. The app does not send marketing push notifications.

How we use your data

We use your data solely to provide the app: tracking time, calculating earnings, generating invoices and reports, syncing your data across devices, and improving the app based on the anonymous usage events. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account). Because the account is shared, this also deletes your BudgetTracker and HabitTracker data. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play. You can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below; we will delete your data within 30 days. When an account is deleted, its usage events are detached from it and can no longer be linked to you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
