Privacy Policy — TimeTracker

Last updated: 29 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TimeTracker mobile application ("the app", "we") handles your data. The app is operated by Martin Antas ("the developer").

What data we collect

When you create an account and use the app, we store:

  • Account data: your email address (for sign-in and account recovery).
  • Your content: the clients, projects, time entries, invoices and settings you create in the app.

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for advertising. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How your data is stored

  • Your account and content are stored securely in our backend provider Supabase (PostgreSQL database with row-level security). Each user can only access their own data.
  • Some preferences (language, Pomodoro and goal settings) are stored locally on your device only.

Payments

Pro subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing, managed through RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat processes a purchase identifier to verify your subscription status.

Notifications

The app may send local notifications (e.g. a running-timer reminder and Pomodoro phase alerts). These are generated on your device. The app does not send marketing push notifications.

How we use your data

We use your data solely to provide the app's functionality: tracking time, calculating earnings, generating invoices and reports, and syncing your data across your sessions. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will delete your data within 30 days of the request.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact
